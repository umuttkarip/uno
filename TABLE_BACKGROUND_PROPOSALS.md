# Masa arka planı önerileri · 6.0.1

Bu görseller yalnız ayrı onay önizlemesidir; oyun seçeneklerine ve otomatik skin eşlemesine eklenmez. Klasik yeşil keçe mevcut native CSS tasarımıyla karşılaştırma referansıdır. Diğer beş yeni görsel built-in ImageGen ile üretildi, orijinaller korunarak proje içine kopyalandı. `scripts/prepare-table-proposals.py` yalnız 1600×900 WebP boyutlandırır/sıkıştırır; yaratıcı görüntü düzenlemesi yapmaz.

Önizleme: `/uno/test/masalar.html?gorunum=ozet`. Masaüstü/telefon ve kartlı/kartsız karşılaştırma var. Sayfa oyun tercihlerini okumaz veya değiştirmez. Her resim yalnız o örnek görüntülendiğinde yüklenir; normal oyun bu klasöre erişmez.

Kaynaklar: `app/.local/table-art-proposals/{theme}.png`. Çıktılar: `app/design-proposals/backgrounds/{cats,seal,kuromi,adventure,minecraft}.webp`. Sude referansı kullanıcının orijinal beyaz üçgen karakteridir; diğerleri yeni ortam çizimleridir. Başka bir model veya CLI yolu kullanılmadı.

## cats

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: a full-screen background for a quiet personal UNO card table, landscape 16:9. Create a refined hand-painted 2D illustrated cat nook interpreted as a playable felt tabletop. Base color dark muted moss teal (#243d34); extremely subtle warm paper/felt grain, flat soft light. Leave the central 75 percent and the entire lower-central hand area nearly uniform and empty so colorful playing cards dominate. Along only the far outer edges: a small naturally proportioned curled sleeping tabby in the lower left, an understated yarn thread running at the very perimeter, a few soft leaf/window shadows near the top edge. Natural drawn cats, never plush toys, never realistic photos. Muted cream and dusty terracotta accents must stay low contrast and subordinate. No UI, text, logos, cards, frames, vignette spotlights, glitter, glowing effects, or busy repeated paws. Calm coherent premium illustrated tabletop, readable on both desktop and center-cropped phone.

## seal

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: landscape 16:9 background for the Sude UNO card skin. Image 1 is an identity/style reference only, not an edit target: the original white triangular fluffy scribble character, tiny lash-heavy black eyes, small pink triangular nose, simple arms. Create a quiet dark dusty mauve/slate tabletop (#443a46) with very subtle matte felt/paper grain. Keep central 75 percent plus lower-central area totally empty, close to uniform; colorful cards will sit there. At the extreme upper-left edge place one small, faint, hand-drawn outline echo of the EXACT reference character, no redesign, no glossy mascot, not a toy. Its lines should be a subdued muted lavender a little lighter than the table and a tiny dusty-pink nose, not bright white. A few short subtle curved scribble marks at far opposite edge can recall the character's little arms. Entire composition deliberately restrained, flat and soft, no focal spotlight. No text, UI, cards, giant faces, hearts, sparkles, gradients of rainbow light, borders or panels. Character is a quiet peripheral detail, not a sticker collage.

## kuromi

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: landscape 16:9 quiet background for a Kuromi themed UNO card table. Dark charcoal-aubergine matte base (#2c2636), fine barely visible fabric grain. Central 75 percent and lower-central playing hand zone must be empty, uniform, low contrast. Use elegant faint gothic Kuromi stationery motifs only at the extreme outer edges: black pointed jester ears and tiny subdued pink skull medallion in one upper corner, soft lavender ribbon and a sparse lace shadow confined to the opposite outer edge. A small recognizable Kuromi silhouette can peek from the far upper-left edge, drawn 2D and understated, not glossy, not a large portrait. Restrained dusty lavender, muted rose, charcoal; almost tone-on-tone so UNO cards command attention. No text, logos, UI, cards, neon, glow, shiny jewels, scattered icon wallpaper, checkerboard, large white areas, or dramatic lights. Premium quiet tabletop rather than a decorative poster; desktop and center-cropped phone usable.

## adventure

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: landscape 16:9 background for an Adventure Time UNO table. Hand-drawn 2D cartoon Land of Ooo interpreted as a very quiet overhead play surface. Deep muted teal-green base (#183c35), flat soft matte grain. Preserve an almost uniform completely empty central 75 percent, including lower-central hand area. Put recognizable Ooo environment hints only along the far perimeter: a tiny Finn and Jake treehouse on a low hill at the far upper-left, gently layered Candy Kingdom silhouettes at far upper-right, a thin winding river following the outer left edge. All distant, desaturated, low contrast, no characters and no focal scene. Use the clear confident cartoon linework and organically simple shapes of Adventure Time, not realistic painting or generic fantasy. No foreground objects, sky horizon across the middle, UI, lettering, cards, frame, glow, beams, glitter or confetti. Cards will be the visually loud elements. Landscape artwork must still feel calm when center cropped to a phone.

## minecraft

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: landscape 16:9 quiet Minecraft themed UNO play-table background. Authentic recognizable Minecraft Overworld block vocabulary in crisp restrained illustrated pixel/voxel art, viewed predominantly from above. Main field is dark muted grass green (#1b392e) with extremely low contrast block texture; CENTRAL 75 PERCENT AND LOWER CENTRAL ZONE EMPTY AND UNIFORM for playing cards. Only at extreme outer edges show a few clear square grass/dirt blocks, a narrow calm blue water corner, a little moss and a tiny pink axolotl by that distant water. True cubic grid, no smooth generic fantasy, no Egyptian architecture or desert monuments. Low contrast environmental hints, subdued natural greens and dusty water blue, matte daylight with almost no directional shadow. No large characters, mobs, bright sky, glowing ore, portal, neon, flames, torches, particle effects, cards, UI, text, decorative framing or light streaks. Designed for sustained concentration, not a splash screen. Usable both wide and center-cropped phone.
