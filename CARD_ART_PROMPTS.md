# Card artwork prompt set

Built-in image_gen, transparent output; exact generation prompts. A sheets provide the first sixteen scenes; B sheets provide eight more numerical scenes and eight action/back illustrations. Source atlas files are retained privately in app/.local/card-art-source/.

## cats-a

Production illustration atlas for a richly illustrated UNO deck, NOT four repeated sticker faces. Exactly 16 SQUARE cells in a strict 4 columns by 4 rows grid, all equal size, read left-to-right top-to-bottom. Square 2048x2048 composition. True transparent background. Each cell contains one complete distinct compact illustrated vignette with 12% transparent padding on every side, no crop, no art crossing cell boundaries, no grid lines. NO lettering, numerals, card frames, UI, logos, or labels; real card numbers and symbols will be placed in code later. Each scene must have a visibly DIFFERENT composition, body pose, action, prop, silhouette and story, not just different eyes. Consistent art direction across all 16. Strong clean outlines and simple color blocks, legible at 90 pixels, no 3D, plush toy, photorealism or excessive detail. Theme: a storybook cast of FOUR recognizably feline domestic cats (gray tabby, ginger, black-and-white tuxedo, calico), matching 2D ink-and-colored-pencil style. Cats have elongated feline bodies, visible haunches, cat paws, triangular ears, whiskers and long expressive tails; NOT round baby dolls with human hands. Anatomically catlike but decidedly drawn cartoon, not realistic. Row 1: gray tabby stretching long after a nap; ginger chasing a rolling yarn ball mid-pounce; tuxedo peering from a cardboard box; calico balancing on a stack of books. Row 2: ginger reaching for a butterfly with one paw; gray tabby curled asleep around its tail; tuxedo fishing a toy mouse from under a cushion; calico leaping through a hoop. Row 3: gray tabby studying a goldfish in a round bowl; ginger caught inside a paper grocery bag; tuxedo climbing a little scratching tower; calico grooming an extended back leg. Row 4: ginger and tabby playfully touching noses; tuxedo stealing a fish-shaped biscuit from a plate; calico sheltering under a giant leaf in rain; gray tabby sliding sideways while chasing a feather. Keep props small and each cat dominant. Warm cream paper-white, gray, ginger, muted pink accents. Natural feline expressions, no uniform big shiny doll eyes. Mature charming illustrated playing-card quality.

Keep the paw held prominently toward us, with the same expression and pose.
